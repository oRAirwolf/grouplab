# Frozen definitions: the zeroing grids as printed until 2026-09-27

These are the four zeroing sheets as they were printed from the first build of the library until NOTES-FROM-PLANNING.md entries 226 and 227 redrew them (grid style 1: a 0.1 mil or 0.5 MOA grid of fine lines, plus or minus 0.8 mil or 2.5 to 3 MOA, four corner codes). Each is named by the identifier printed on the sheet, and each is still identified when a printout of it is scanned.

**They are never edited.** A sheet printed from one of them is read against it, and its identifier changes whenever its geometry does. `BuiltInLibraryTests` holds each to the reference tools' body and identifier and to its old geometry in `tools/layout/layouts.json`.
