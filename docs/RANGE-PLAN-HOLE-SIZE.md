# Range plan: the hole size test (Program B)

One page to print and take to the range. NOTES-FROM-PLANNING.md entry 235; the reasons are in `docs/RESEARCH.md`, Program B.

**Print:** 10 sheets of GL-CF25-LTR-D, the 25 bull Letter load sheet, all at once on one printer and one paper, at 100 percent.
Eight are used, two are spare.

**Set up:** one backing for every sheet, each stapled the same way; 50 yards; the rifle zeroed first, so every shot lands on its own bull.

**Shoot, in this order, one shot to a bull, 25 shots a sheet, aimed at each bull's center:**

1. .22 LR subsonic
2. .300 Blackout subsonic
3. .22 LR high velocity
4. 6.5 Creedmoor (or 6 ARC)
5. .22 LR subsonic
6. .300 Blackout subsonic
7. .22 LR high velocity
8. 6.5 Creedmoor (or 6 ARC)

That is the whole core set: eight sheets, two of each. **Only if there is time,** two sheets each, alternated in the same way: 8.6
Blackout subsonic, .510 Whisper, and whichever of 6 ARC and 6.5 Creedmoor was not shot above.

**Write on each sheet's load block:** the date; 50 yd; the cartridge; the bullet's weight and type; the powder and charge, or the maker if
factory; the velocity if a chronograph was up; and in the serial box, the sheet's number from the list above.

**Afterwards:** scan every sheet flat on the Brother flatbed at 600 dpi, lid closed, one sheet a scan, and name each file by its sheet
number. Sheets that are not GroupLab's are photographed instead, square on, in even light.
