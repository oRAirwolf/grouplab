## 4. How typed text is read

A diameter typed as a decimal below one, such as .308 or 0.308, or any number marked in inches, is always read exactly as typed. Otherwise a name from part 1 or part 2 gives its diameter, and a name that means more than one diameter, part 3, shows its candidates and asks. Anything else falls to the leading-number guess: a number from 1 to 14 is read as millimetres, from 14 to 100 as hundredths of an inch and from 100 to 1000 as thousandths, and GroupLab says what it read so a wrong guess can be corrected by typing the diameter.
